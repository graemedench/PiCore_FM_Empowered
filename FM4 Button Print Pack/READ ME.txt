   A visual guide to every selectable cap in the all-variants project.

   One current-generation blank cap, centred on its own plate and printed entirely with filament 3.

INDIVIDUAL PARTS

4. Individual STL Files\
   All individual cap parts are together in one folder. Every regular cap is a Body + White pair: Body = filament 3 (dark); White = filament 2 (white).
   The revised Play/Pause and Play Mode caps are included in both Flush and Raised 0.2 mm versions in the same format. The two *_White_Overlay files are retained only for advanced custom projects; use the matching Body + White files for normal printing.

5. Resin - Single Colour Raised 1mm\
   Ten one-piece resin-printing button STL files. Each is a single colour with its icon raised 1 mm above the cap face; no separate body or white part is needed. Blank_-_Resin_Single_Colour.stl is the matching plain no-icon cap.

6. Blank Cap\blank button.ipt
   The supplied blank Inventor source and STL, retained for design work. It is an earlier blank-cap source; use Quadify_Blank_Current_Cap.3mf when printing a current fitted blank cap.

Re-slice each project in Creality Print before printing. Development and earlier button sets remain outside this final-pack folder.
