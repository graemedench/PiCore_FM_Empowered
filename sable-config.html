Streaming accounts

BBC SoundsSign in and manage BBC Sounds. TIDALLink your TIDAL account. Beta SpotifySpotOn account and playback authorisation. Spotify Premium required. Beta QobuzSign in with your Qobuz account and streaming subscription.

Choose which services appear on the OLED in Settings → Streaming Services. Hiding a service keeps its account settings.

Spotify remote sign-in: if your browser lands on an unavailable 127.0.0.1 page, copy its complete URL into SpotOn's Manual authorisation field. A “429 Too Many Requests” error means Spotify is rate limiting requests; wait before retrying. Spotify and Qobuz playback are not yet verified in this beta.
